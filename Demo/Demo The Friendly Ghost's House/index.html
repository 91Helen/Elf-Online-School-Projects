🏰 The Friendly Ghost's House

Learn English with Boo! DEMO

❓ How to play

Boo is a friendly ghost. Help him decorate his castle and say what's in the room in English! The game practises There is / There are, How many…? and Is / Are there…?. Turn on the sound. Microphone (Chrome / Edge) is optional: the child repeats the phrase aloud.

  1. Level 1 — How many…? Count and tap the number.
  2. Level 2 — Is / Are there…? Look at the room, answer Yes or No.
  3. Levels 3–5 (toys, sentence building, prepositions, listen & move) — in the full version 🔓

🔊 repeat · 👁️ hide text · 🎵 music · ⭐ 2 stars for a first-try answer, 1 for a retry, +1 for saying the phrase.
